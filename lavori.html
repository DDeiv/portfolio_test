Personal project: a minimalist browser arcade game (RECOIL).
Software used: JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Claude AI.

Featuring: physics-based movement, mouse/keyboard and touch controls, score system, invertible color scheme, full-screen responsive canvas.

The core mechanic is built around a single constraint: the ship has no thrusters, and the only way to move is the recoil of your own shots. Aiming, moving, and shooting become the same gesture, which makes survival a constant trade-off between dodging and firing. It runs entirely in the browser, on desktop and mobile.

Personal project: an editorial-style care manual for my houseplants (The Botanical Editorial).
Software used: JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Claude AI.

Featuring: master reference table, individual care monographs, recovery protocols, weekly and monthly routine checklists with saved progress, PDF export, responsive design.

Built to keep all of my plants alive in one place: each of the 12 species has its own monograph with light and watering protocols (and a "drama level" rating), while recovery sections cover what to do when something goes wrong. The routine schedule tracks weekly and monthly tasks and remembers progress between visits.

Soup.fm is a collective that I cofounded in 2024 with a friend.

The project was for us an occasion to experiment with different things, from curating music to organizing events and sharing art. In 2024 and 2025, we gathered an estimated 6,000 people in Lisbon across multiple events. We mainly communicate via WhatsApp; we only decided to share it on Instagram before our (for now) last event. The total following across all platforms is still around 2,500 people.

Out of respect for the privacy of the people who attended our events, I won't share any pictures or videos of them; some are, nonetheless, visible on the Instagram page.
